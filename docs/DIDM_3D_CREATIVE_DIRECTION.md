## 1. Visual World Philosophy

We reject the generic tropes of "cheap gaming interfaces", "neon cyber overload", and "meaningless floating cubes". 
Instead, DIDM's 3D universe is conceived as an **architectural futuristic academy**—a stylized digital campus where each learning lab corresponds directly to verified DIDM curriculum modules:

```
                          [ Analytics Observatory ]
                                    │
    [ SEO Discovery Lab ] ── [ Central Learning Hub ] ── [ Advertising Command Centre ]
            │                       │                               │
    [ Social Media Studio ] ────────┴─────────────── [ AI Innovation Lab ]
                                    │
                        [ 650+ Placement Beacons ]
```

### The 5 Thematic Learning Destinations

1. **Central Learning Hub:**
   - Architectural glass and crimson pavilion with rotating double-torus gold beacon.
   - Represents DIDM's decade-long foundation (estd. 2014, 50k+ alumni).
2. **SEO Discovery Lab:**
   - Ascending geometric pillars representing algorithmic search ranking factors, keyword crawl graphs, and on-page audit metrics.
3. **Social Media Studio:**
   - Curved architectural screen wall with content wave distribution node and viral broadcasting orb.
4. **Advertising Command Centre:**
   - Performance marketing funnel cylinder with target rings, symbolizing Google Search, Shopping, YouTube, and Meta Advantage+ campaigns.
5. **AI Innovation Lab:**
   - Floating neural icosahedron core, symbolizing Midjourney ad creative generation, Claude copywriting pipelines, and generative AI automation.
6. **Analytics Observatory:**
   - Precision data prism with emerald attribution facet, symbolizing GA4 event streams and full-funnel conversion tracking.

## 2. Stylized Student Character Design

- **Concept:** College-age and young professional learners wearing contemporary smart-casual apparel (DIDM varsity jacket, dark denim, white trainers, backpack).
- **Animation System:**
  - Forward kinematics with harmonic sine/cosine interpolation.
  - Realistic leg/arm counter-swing ($\theta = A \sin(\omega t)$).
  - Subtle vertical hip bobbing and breathing motion.
  - Observational head turns and tablet/analytics interaction gestures.
- **Stylization Principle:** Original stylized 3D geometry rather than photorealistic human avatars, ensuring respect for real DIDM students while conveying vibrant human energy.
