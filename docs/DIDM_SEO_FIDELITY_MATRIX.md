## 2. Meta Description & Social OpenGraph Standards

Every page specifies:
- **Title Tag:** Distinct, keyword-rich, and branded with `- DIDM`.
- **Meta Description:** Concise (140-160 chars), educational value proposition, branch count, and clear CTA.
- **OpenGraph:** `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:type="website"`, `og:image` (pointing to verified official logo or course cover), `og:url` (canonical).
- **Twitter Card:** `summary_large_image` with brand social tags.
- **Robots Directives:** `index: true, follow: true` for all public canonical routes; `index: false, follow: false` for admin and dynamic internal utilities.
