# DIDM Interactive Training Institute — Experience Audit & Correction Plan

**Date:** November 5, 2026  
**Status:** In Progress — Major UX & Educational Experience Realignment  
**Guiding Vision:** World-class digital marketing training institute (Kraftshala pedagogical clarity, Apple editorial elegance, Wispr Flow fluidity).  

---

## 1. Critical Audit: What Must Be Removed Immediately

| Problematic Element | Why It Failed | Action Taken |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Floating ROAS & Ad Spend Cards** | Felt like an e-commerce marketing SaaS analytics dashboard rather than a physical classroom training institute with 14 campuses. | **Removed.** Replaced with authentic admissions value propositions, verified curriculum highlights, and real student learning environments. |
| **Simulated Unverified SEO Rankings** | Claims like "Rank #1 Position / 94 Topical Authority" were decorative and risked misrepresenting verified institutional facts. | **Removed.** Replaced with verified curriculum topics (entity SEO, schema markup, Core Web Vitals). |
| **Floating AI Multi-Agent Boxes** | Cluttered the hero canvas with generic tech buzzwords. | **Removed.** Refactored into a dedicated, grounded **AI Course Counsellor** chat assistant. |
| **Excessive 3D Tilt & Glare** | Distracting tilt effects created motion sickness and detracted from editorial legibility. | **Toned Down.** Replaced with clean, refined micro-elevation, subtle border transitions, and purposeful reveals. |
| **Generic Course Card Grids** | Did not visually convey the rich week-by-week learning journey students experience across 50+ modules. | **Replaced.** Building a unique **Interactive Course Roadmap** with a travelling student avatar for every course. |

---

## 2. The New Design Direction

1. **Warm, Academic & Authoritative Canvas:** Clean off-white and charcoal surfaces, high-contrast typography, restrained DIDM red accents (`#C8102E`), and spacious layouts.
2. **Every Course Has Its Own Roadmap:** Instead of static syllabus lists, every program detail page features an SVG learning roadmap with an illustrated student character walking from checkpoint to checkpoint as candidates explore modules.
3. **Grounded AI Course Counsellor:** A floating conversational assistant strictly grounded in DIDM's verified courses, centres, syllabi, and admissions details.
4. **Consistent Educational Motion:** Purposeful transitions on all public pages (programs, centres, about, outcomes, stories, organisations, contact, privacy).
