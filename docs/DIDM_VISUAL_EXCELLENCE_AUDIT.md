# Visual excellence audit and priority matrix

Assessment: 17 November 2026 (Asia/Kolkata). Local implementation only. No deployment, migration or authenticated database mutation.

## Scope and evidence
All 14 reports linked by DIDM_IMPLEMENTATION_SUMMARY.md were read, prioritising readiness, gap, regression, design and motion reports. Historical screenshots, QA JSON and build evidence were inspected and treated as a baseline, not proof of current success. Fresh source inventory identifies **39 public page files/templates**, reusable layouts and **1,083 generated public URL variants** (including aliases). The 1,001 news variants use a shared detail template; only two news content instances received screenshot review. They were NOT all individually fact-checked.

Fresh browser coverage: **186 before captures** across 62 routes and **201 after captures** across 67 routes, each at 1440, 768 and 390 pixels; seven priority routes also have desktop detail captures. All 14 centres and eight recognition-detail variants are included. New story details have after-only evidence; their prior counterpart is /stories. Every indexed public URL received a read-only HTTP/H1 check. This separates route/template coverage from editorial approval of every content record.

Artifacts: [inventory](phase-2/page-inventory.json), [all route checks](phase-2/public-route-check.json), [before metrics](phase-2/visual-before.json), [after metrics](phase-2/visual-after.json), [side-by-side reviewer](phase-2/visual-comparison.html).

## Design decision
Keep the established DIDM red, warm paper, Poppins/Open Sans and editorial typography. Extend it through a scoped public shell; admin/private workflows are excluded. Give readable HTML copy precedence over embedded-text artwork. Use original local photography with accurate archive captions, not invented course associations. Keep original artwork accessible in disclosure controls. Reuse existing program identities, catalog, source images and working learning tools.

## Assessment dimensions and honest limits
The captures and DOM metrics cover every listed template; visual inspection concentrated on the changed hero, course, contact, placement, gallery and navigation states. This is not a manual pixel-by-pixel sign-off of all 201 screens or factual approval of the news archive.

- Hierarchy/typography: title before source artwork; shared heading scale and restrained editorial accent; supporting plain pages receive the same rhythm.
- Whitespace/color/brand: bounded content widths, quiet borders, consistent controls and corrected dark-panel text. Large source galleries progressively disclose rather than occupy the whole page.
- Imagery: local archive sources retained; two failed remote images resolve to their exact local artifacts. No new stock testimonial or hiring logo introduced. Media consent/claims remain approval gates.
- Interaction/conversion: all four required student journeys tested; actual links, filters, controls, failed-form recovery and human handoff used. Success responses are intercepted, not real lead persistence.
- Responsiveness: 201 after captures show no document overflow or visible unnamed form controls. Curriculum map scrolls inside its own region, with touch targets at least 26 pixels.
- Motion: page-load scroll jump removed; arrow handling is scoped to curriculum controls; scroll entrances are fail-open and cancel for reduced motion. Existing hero/campaign storytelling and student-map movement remain. No new WebGL scene or animation dependency.
- Accessibility: programmatic fields, source-matched names, dialog focus/restore, headings, contrast and native controls are checked. Automated WCAG rules are not full WCAG 2.2 AA certification; external player state and assistive-device/manual checks remain.
- Accuracy: five quotes match the captured official source. This establishes publication evidence only. Unsupported public score decorations and salary narratives removed from the affected journeys; broader claim approval is still open.
