FAQ'S

  • What if I missed some classes? How to cover the missed classes? What are the charges for the same?

    The missed classes can be covered by attending any of the upcoming batches, which can be considered as back-up classes. DIDM does not charge any extra fee for the same. However, this has to be pre-informed to the support team.

  • I did not understand some topics of the program. Is it possible to cover the topics once again? If yes, then is there any extra charge for it?

    Yes, you can get access to the upcoming batches to form and have a better understanding. DIDM never charges any extra fee for doubt clearing session.

  • What is the salary that I will get after completing the Digital Marketing program?

    You can get a salary from Rs. 10000 to Rs. 50,000 monthly depending on your skill and calibre

  • Do you provide training material?

    Yes, DIDM provides training material in the form of E-notes and tools.

  • What are the criteria to get a confirmed status?

    We have an evaluation system that judges trainees based on elective subject and practical knowledge. We provide the certification once you satisfy the criteria and complete the course program.

  • What are the chances of working from home after completing the course? Is there any chance to get projects that I can complete from home?

    Once you complete the course, you have the option to work as a freelancer, for which we’ve a complete module of freelancer push-ups. With this, you earn a handsome amount from the comfort of your home.

  • Do you provide training material?

    Yes, DIDM provides training material in the form of soft copies.

  • Will DIDM help find a job after the completion of training?

    Yes, DIDM assures 100% job assistance to all its trainees.

  • How flexible is the schedule? When do you conduct classes?

    DIDM conduct classes on weekends, weekdays along with online classes at evening and night. We also entertain trainees requests, especially the employed ones.

  • Is there any chance to find job opportunities offshore after the course?

    Indeed,one will have ample opportunities to choose from. After our Melbourne branch we have join hands with more companies off-shore.
