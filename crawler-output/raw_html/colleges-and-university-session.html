FAQ

  • Who can opt for this course?

    1. Students (Any Stream), 2. Interns Faculty, 3. Management Team

  • Why should digital marketing be introduced in educational institutions?

    Digital marketing is of great value to modern businesses. It helps you reach an unexplored market of customers. It is now clear that due to the internet, people always look for products and services online first. So, it is important to advertise your brand online using various digital marketing techniques. As it is a booming industry, learning digital skills will add weightage to the students’ curriculum vitae.

  • What will be the duration of the course?

    It depends on which course you opt for. We have courses that last a few weeks to even 6 months.

  • What modules will be taught?

    Generally, we teach 50+ modules in our institute. You can opt for the entire course or you can choose according to your interest. Ice Breaker for Digital Marketing, Digital Marketing Strategy, Web Designing with no codes, Practical Approach, Email Marketing, Inbound Marketing, Search Engine Optimization, Google Webmaster Tool, Search Engine Algorithms, Local Business Listing, Google Analytics, Content Marketing, Competitors Analysis, Social Media Marketing, Video Marketing Fundamentals, Media Buying and Planning, E-commerce Marketing, Mobile Marketing, Optimization Conversion rate, Google Adwords, Online Display Advertising, Online Reputation Management, Creating Marketing Strategy, Lead Generation for Business, Live Practical Session, Case Study Discussion, Digital Marketing, Blogging, AdSense, Affiliate Marketing, Freelance Pushups, Black Hat Techniques, Search Engine and Social Marketing Parameters and Remarketing Concepts.

  • What are the career opportunities?

    Students can hone their digital skills and go for job profiles like SEO executive, SMO specialist, business analyst, digital marketing manager, email marketing specialist etc.
