FAQ

  • What is the importance of corporate training?

    The importance of corporate training is to ensure that all our candidates can handle their projects well when it comes to fulfilling the company goals and objectives. Continued training in an individual career has become a huge part of the corporate culture. We at DIDM help our students get their dream job by providing hands-on experience in digital marketing so they can grab the opportunity once they step out of our institute.

  • I have experience in Traditional Marketing. Can I shift my career to digital marketing?

    Yes, of course. In today’s digital environment, traditional marketers must understand the concept of digital marketing while running campaigns for clients in digital media to get good ROI.

  • I am not an engineer neither do I possess Programming knowledge. Will I be able to do this Digital Marketing course?

    You need not know technology at all or come from an engineering/technical background to be successful in Digital Marketing .

  • I am a Marketing Professional with 5+ years of experience. Should I learn Digital Marketing?

    It’s an excellent course to take up if you want to stay in a marketing role. With the growing contribution of digital marketing, marketing is becoming integrated, and every marketing professional (including PR/Media/Content) must possess knowledge. Build a personal brand using digital marketing. Make money through freelancing or by becoming an affiliate marketer. Realize your entrepreneurial dream if you aspire to be an entrepreneur. Also, explore the possibility of playing a role in the marketing function of your organization to expand your profile.

  • Is the course theoretical or practical?

    Our training program will be a combination of theoretical and practical in each module. We also provide live exposure on projects, and we also give assignments to test your skill sets.

  • Can I work as a freelancer after completing the course?

    Yes, our course helps you learn every concept in real-time. At the end of the sessions, we will discuss various concepts of Freelancing to help you get started in this industry.

  • What is the mode of training?

    We have 2 modes of training: Classroom Training & Online Training.

  • Are your online training sessions interactive?

    Yes, we have Q&A sessions, small group discussions, and other interactive elements in the session to ensure quality learning.

  • What is the salary for professionals in Digital Marketing?

    At the entry-level, a student or a fresher who may not be even a graduate starts his/her career as a Digital Marketing Executive and earns between Rs 1.5 - 4 Lacs salary per annum in India. At a Digital Marketing Head position, salary rises to as high as Rs 30 Lacs. Here’s the salary range based on profiles and years of experience: For professionals with exceptional educational qualification (e.g. from IITs/IIMs), with highly relevant work experience and excellent command over digital marketing, high growth start-ups, large corporations, and platform companies such as Flipkart, Amazon, Google, Facebook, LinkedIn offer as high as Rs. 1 crore salary.

  • Can I train my office employees through your corporate digital marketing program?

    Yes, everyone can learn Digital Marketing for fulfilling various needs and requirements of the organization
