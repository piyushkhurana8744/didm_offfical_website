Digital Marketing Intelligence (DMI) Diploma Program

The Digital Marketing Intelligence (DMI) course is India's first 1-year AI-integrated diploma program designed to transition students from traditional marketers into growth strategists. Spanning over 400 hours, the curriculum operates on a unique "Intelligence Cycle" that builds proficiency through a repetitive loop of academy training, assessments, mentorship on an active agency floor, and live projects with real brands. The syllabus covers seven distinct phases, ranging from Digital Architecture and AI Genesis to Performance Matrix and E-Commerce Strategic Intelligence, ensuring mastery over tools like Gemini, SEMrush, and Google Ads. To ensure career readiness, the program includes exclusive benefits such as a Pre-Placement Offer (PPO), a 2-month paid internship, 3 months of agency floor experience, and funding to run real paid ad campaigns.

Your 1-year Evolution

Delhi Institute of Digital Marketing (DIDM) Covers 07 Chapters that you will learn in the Digital Marketing Intelligence (DMI) program
 
MONTH 1-2: THE DIGITAL ARCHITECT

i) The Mission: Laying the foundation. You don’t just browse the web; you learn to build it.

ii) What You Learn: Digital Architecture, AI Genesis Tools, and Consumer Psychology.

iii)The Milestone: You launch your own Self-Portfolio Website and create your first AI-driven content strategy.

MONTH 3-4 : THE ORGANIC GROWTH HACKER

i) The Mission: Getting discovered. You master the art of making algorithms work for you without spending a rupee.

ii) What You Learn: Search Intelligence, SEO (On-Page/Off-Page), and Local Dominance.

iii)The Milestone: You perform a 360-degree SEO Performance Audit for a live brand and execute a blog ranking strategy.

MONTH 5-6 : THE VIRAL ENGINEER

i) The Mission: Creating the buzz. You learn the science behind what makes content go viral.

ii) What You Learn: Social Ecosystems, Viral Engineering, and Influencer Outreach.

iii)The Milestone: You execute a Lead Generation Campaign and manage a full social media calendar for 30+ days.

MONTH 7-8 : THE PERFORMANCE MARKETER

i) The Mission: Scaling with ROI. You move from organic traffic to paid ad dominance using real money.

ii) What You Learn: The Performance Matrix, Google Ads Mastery, and Conversion optimization.

iii)The Milestone: You step onto the Agency Floor to manage a Live Budgeted Ad campaign with real client briefs.

MONTH 9-10 : THE STRATEGIST & FREELANCER

i) The Mission: The business of marketing. You learn to pitch, price, and present like a pro.

ii) What You Learn: Strategic Intelligence, Funnel Marketing, and Client Proposal Creation.

iii)The Milestone: Freelancing Simulation—you pitch to a client and build a resume that demands attention.

MONTH 11-12 : THE MASTER SPECIALIST

i) The Mission: The Final Polish. You choose your specific career path.

ii) What You Learn: Deep-dive specialization in AI-SEO, Social Growth, or Performance Marketing.

iii)The Milestone: You earn your 3-Month Experience Certificate and secure your Pre-Placement Offer (PPO).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

  • What is the duration of the DMI program?

    The program is a comprehensive, 1-year professional diploma spanning 52 weeks.

  • How much practical training is included?

    The course includes over 400 hours of intensive, hands-on training dedicated to mastering tools and execution.

  • Does this course cover Artificial Intelligence?

    Yes, the curriculum includes "Al-Genesis," which focuses on the integration of Generative AI and AI-driven marketing strategies.

  • What is the "Agency Floor" mentioned in the brochure?

    The Agency Floor is a 3-month period where students gain real-time experience working with real budgets and clients, earning an experience certificate upon completion.

  • Is there a paid internship included?

    Yes, the program features a 2-month paid internship where students gain on-the-job experience and earn a stipend while they study.

  • Will I work on live projects?

    Absolutely. Students manage real campaigns for actual brands across various industries like E-commerce, Real Estate, and EdTech.

  • What is a PPO, and do I get one?

    A PPO is a Pre-Placement Offer. Top performers have the chance to secure guaranteed employment from partner agencies before the course even ends.

  • What certifications will I receive?

    Students can earn over 35 certifications with global recognition from partners like Google, HubSpot, and MSME (Skill India).

  • Does DIDM provide placement support?

    Yes, there are dedicated sessions for resume building, mock interviews, and direct access to hiring partners.

  • Are tools provided during the course?

    Students get access to over 30 premium paid tools and a "DMI Power Kit" which includes a laptop bag, diary, and pen.

  • Do I get any help with web hosting?

    Yes, the program includes a 1-year web hosting plan for free to help you launch your own projects.
